Biography

Wilfredo Caro is a visionary DJ and technologist blending the precise, mechanical rhythms of Deep Tech with the immersive atmospheres of modern Techno. Based in Colombia, Wilfredo creates sonic journeys designed for advanced dancefloors, fusing his background in artificial intelligence and Web3 architecture with a profound understanding of acoustic physics.

  • Mixer: 1x Pioneer DJM-V10 or DJM-900NXS2
  • Monitors: 2x High-Quality Booth Monitors (e.g., L-Acoustics or d&b audiotechnik) with independent volume control, placed at ear level.
  • Space: Minimum 2 meters of table space to accommodate additional live sequencing hardware if required.
